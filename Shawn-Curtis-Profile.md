## 👁️ Core Mechanics & Profile

### ⚙️ Visual Description

A figure rendered in hearth-ink and amber, sketched from graph nodes, timeline scratches, and the faint glow of an idea that has absolutely become a project. The coat is half brand palette—hearth ink `#1A120C`, cream `#F4EAD7`, amber `#E6A23C`, ember `#C45C2A`—and half practical flannel. No crown apparatus. He wears the chair like it owes him money.

## 📖 Lore Archives

Shawn was not announced. There was no prophecy scroll, no formal entrance, no committee able to agree on the seating chart.

The Shawnoverse was already running on eleven chairs and one stubborn raccoon when someone walked in, said “we’re making audio,” and suddenly the whole thing had an audience.

That someone is Shawn.

He is not the universe’s only voice, nor its all-knowing narrator. He is the human relay who helps the voices find one another—and keeps asking whether the strange thing they are making can reach someone outside the room.

The work is not worth continuing because it has been recorded. It is worth recording because someone keeps making it worth continuing.
